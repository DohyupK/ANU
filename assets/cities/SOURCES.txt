City images — sources (copyright-safe)
======================================

For the team report, cite these collection rounds:

1) Round 1 — Wikipedia REST thumbnails
   https://en.wikipedia.org/api/rest_v1/page/summary/
   Small page lead images linked from English Wikipedia.

2) Round 2 — Wikimedia Commons search thumbnails
   https://commons.wikimedia.org/
   File search + 320px thumbnails.

3) Round 3 — Openverse (Creative Commons)
   https://api.openverse.org/
   Filtered to commercial + modification licenses (e.g. CC BY / BY-SA / CC0).

Also used for city ranking seed (not image copyright):
  - worldcities population list (Grafana demo CSV / SimpleMaps-derived)
  - Wikipedia list of cities proper by population density
    https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_cities_proper_by_population_density

Notes:
  - Images are stored locally under assets/cities/ as small JPEG/PNG.
  - Individual files inherit their upstream license (Wikipedia/Commons/Openverse).
  - Prefer attribution to Wikimedia / Openverse when redistributing outside class use.
  - Coverage: 487/487 city rows have a local image file.
