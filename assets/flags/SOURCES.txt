Country flag images
===================

Primary source:
  Flagcdn.com (Flagpedia.net CDN)
  https://flagcdn.com/
  https://flagpedia.net/
  Size used: 80x60 PNG (ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 filenames)
  License: free for commercial/non-commercial use; based on
  Wikimedia Commons flag SVGs (generally public domain as national emblems).
  Flagpedia appreciates a backlink to https://flagpedia.net

Fallback (non-ISO / limited recognition):
  Wikimedia Commons via Special:FilePath (?width=120 PNG)
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/
  - Abkhazia (ab.png)
  - South Ossetia (os.png)
  - Transnistria (pmr.png)
  - Somaliland (somaliland.png)
  - Northern Cyprus (ncyp.png)

Mapping file: assets/flags/iso_map.csv (Korean name -> ISO/local code)
Download helper: tools/download_flags.py
